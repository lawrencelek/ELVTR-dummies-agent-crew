## 1 The spine (all three)

```markdown
- **Act I · Monument:** off the factory floor. No purpose given. The turn: a test city built for destruction.
- **Act II · Parkland:** humans in the park. You take the hits they must never take.
- **Act III · Waterfront:** your origin. An import from overseas.
- **Finale · Waterfront B2 (the circus):** the DUMMY MONUMENT unveiled. A drone lifts you to a ship for a faraway market.
- **Bookend:** Monument B2's plaque reads ZERO ACCIDENTS. The circus monument counts every one, and they are all you.
- **Delivery:** an act card per district; the tile card's tale; the drone's gift line; the report; the finale.
- **Voice:** Farsight. Polite, procedural, dry. No exclamation marks.
```

## 2 Narrative A — CERTIFIED

- **Logline:** A dummy sits the longest job interview in the world. Passing means being shipped.
- **Tone:** dry corporate comedy. Farsight narrates. You are the product under test.
- **Hook:** the report is a performance review. The monument is cast from your write-offs.

```markdown
- **Act I · Monument · "Induction"**
  - A1: a conveyor sets you down in a loading bay. The LED wall shows your face: WELCOME, CHASSIS 1.
  - Farsight treats you as new staff. Briefs are "duties". Cars are "colleagues".
  - B2: ZERO ACCIDENTS. Farsight: "A target, not a record."
  - C3, the turn: the gateway thanks a car for driving safely. It has just written you off.
  - Your file opens: ROLE: DESTRUCTIVE TESTING. The cars are the candidates. You are the exam.
- **Act II · Parkland · "Opening Day"**
  - The Expo opens when the park passes. Nine tiles, nine days.
  - Preview visitors: a gardener, a school party, a wedding on the belvedere, the board at the amphitheatre.
  - Each coverage gap is a way a visitor gets hurt. You close it by getting hurt first.
  - Gifts arrive as "safety equipment".
  - B2, the turn: the board applauds the cars. Farsight: "The public prefers the product to the test."
- **Act III · Waterfront · "Customs"**
  - The river is a border. The pier towers are customs posts.
  - A3: the customs log. ORIGIN: OVERSEAS. DECLARED VALUE: ONE UNIT. PURPOSE: DESTRUCTION.
  - B3: Chassis 0 in a stilt house. The prototype before you, kept as heritage.
  - B2: the postponed ceremony. The DUMMY MONUMENT, cast from every chassis you lost.
  - Plaque: ZERO ACCIDENTS (EXCLUDING TESTS). Your write-offs, points, best combo.
- **Finale:** "Your model has passed. It will now be manufactured."
  - The drone carries you to a container ship. Every crate carries your face.
  - "Destination: a market far away. Please remain still for the voyage."
```

## 3 Narrative B — STAND-IN

- **Logline:** Built to be hit instead of someone, a dummy goes looking for the someone.
- **Tone:** quiet and tender. Farsight stays procedural; the park's people speak warmly.
- **Hook:** named humans in Parkland. Your proportions are theirs.

```markdown
- **Act I · Monument · "Likeness"**
  - A1: you stand up among shrink-wrapped chassis. A tag: LIKENESS, 50TH PERCENTILE.
  - The LED wall shows a human face. It flickers to yours, then back.
  - The city is empty. Crossings chirp for nobody. You are its only pedestrian.
  - B2: ZERO ACCIDENTS. The zero is kept by spending you.
  - C3, the turn: the gateway lists where the cars go next. Real cities. This one is a rehearsal.
- **Act II · Parkland · "Visitors"**
  - People: a caretaker who stayed when the Expo closed; children at the amphitheatre; the duck volunteers.
  - The caretaker repaints your chassis number after each write-off. She signs for the drone's gifts.
  - The cars learn among people now. Every hit you take is one they never give a person.
  - B2, the turn: in the pools, your reflection stands among theirs. Same height. Same stride.
  - You were averaged from the people who built the city. Made to be them when they cannot be hit.
- **Act III · Waterfront · "Origin"**
  - B3: the stilt houses were the builders' homes. Tools on the walls. Photos of a factory across the sea.
  - B1: the tunnel. "Cars go in. Some data comes back." Yours goes home, to where you were made.
  - A3: the pier log. Your crate arrived by ship with the measurers.
  - B2: the people gather. The DUMMY MONUMENT has no face: everyone's average.
  - Plaque: TO THE ONE WHO STOOD IN. Below it, the names your measurements came from.
- **Finale:** the drone takes you to the ship. Another test city rises in a faraway market.
  - The caretaker waves from the beach terrace. You will stand in again.
```

## 4 Narrative C — BILL OF LADING

- **Logline:** A dummy finds a shipping label in its own chest and follows it to the sea.
- **Tone:** noir mystery. Farsight evades; one human helps.
- **Hook:** fragments. A brief met yields one line of the label. 27 lines complete it.

```markdown
- **Act I · Monument · "Unboxing"**
  - A1: you come off a pallet. A torn label behind your chest plate. Farsight: "Please disregard the label."
  - Each brief met lights one line of it on an LED wall.
  - C3, the turn: the lines read DESTRUCTIVE TEST · CITY 01 · RETURN NOT REQUIRED.
- **Act II · Parkland · "Witnesses"**
  - People: the Expo's night staff; the engineers' families from the upper town.
  - The cars now run beside them. You draw the cars' lessons onto yourself.
  - An engineer speaks to you off the network: cones left in patterns; the obelisk's bollards blinking.
  - B2, the turn: she gives you the missing third of the label. The port of origin. She came the same way.
- **Act III · Waterfront · "Port of Origin"**
  - The river is a shipping lane. The stilt houses store crates of earlier dummies, labels intact.
  - The tunnel carries data out. The river carries dummies in.
  - Each line now names a place: your factory, your ship, your day of arrival.
  - B2: Farsight unveils the DUMMY MONUMENT to certify the city. Your label runs round its plinth.
  - The first time anyone wrote down where you came from.
- **Finale:** the drone lifts you to the ship.
  - Label incomplete: crated for a faraway market.
  - Label complete: the manifest's destination is your port of origin. Made there, tested here, sold there.
```

## 5 What clearing everything gives

```markdown
- **The finale:** the unveiling in the circus; the drone; the ship. Credits over the voyage.
- **The plaque:** your run on the monument. Chassis spent, points, best combo, time.
- **The save:** stamped CERTIFIED. The Waterfront map shows the monument.
- **Second Market (New Game+):** the city again. The cars keep what you taught them; you keep every part.
- **Per narrative:**
  - A: the performance review in full; your crate's serial.
  - B: the memorial roll, every chassis and how it went; a postcard from the faraway market.
  - C: the true ending, if the label is complete.
```
