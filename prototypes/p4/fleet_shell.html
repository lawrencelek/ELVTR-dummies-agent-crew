Fleet mock-up · P4

The eight canon classes at their V29 registry sizes, plus buses at three lengths. Sprites at game scale (1 art px = 2 world units), shown 4×, on 16 px tiles and two 56-unit lanes. Then the swept path of each vehicle through P4's bends.

Line-up

Tile = 16 art px = 32 world units = 2.6 m (proto.scale.unitsPerMetre 12.2). Lane = 56 units = 4.6 m. Widths run about 1.25× real, for readability.

Sizes

In lane: the least inner-lane radius at which the body stays inside its lane through a bend (centre of the body on the lane line, as the sim poses cars). Road centre = that + half a lane. P4 bends: centre 64 (inner lane 36); roundabout entries 96 (inner lane 68).

Swept path

Ghost footprints every 12 units through a 90° bend, both lanes. Red corners stand outside their lane; past the centre line they reach oncoming traffic.

Turn Lab with the expanded fleet

From node fleet_run.js: 180 s per run, P4 driver, dummy away. Pass: no jam, no bodies overlapping, bus overhang ≤ 8. Expanded fleet: one car every 2.5 s from all eight classes; buses only: one every 4 s.
