Traffic

Driver P3: a car judges other cars in a straight strip ahead of its nose. On a bend that strip points at oncoming traffic, and both cars stop. Driver P4: other cars count only where they stand on this car's own lane; a jammed car backs up; at a roundabout entry a car gives way to circulating traffic, and circulating cars let a car in once it is going.
