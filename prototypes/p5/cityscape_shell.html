Three cityscapes · P5 mock-up

Five districts become three, each with its own core: a park under elevated roundabouts, a four-way roundabout, and a monument where streets meet. The centre of each in game art, with live P5 traffic. The P5 districts are unchanged until these are approved.

The districts

Each district stays 3 × 3 screens. Cyan: a Z1 highway row. Violet: Z2. Dashed: the ground road network (illustrative). Green cells: parkland. Magenta (Monument): pedestrian streets meeting at the plaza.

The centrepieces

Highways at P5.2's width (160), with paired lamps and round columns. Elevated roundabouts run one way round, anticlockwise on screen, entered on fillets of world.roundaboutEntryRadius (96). Colours follow the style guide: no red or yellow décor, so the photos' warm light becomes cyan and magenta here.
