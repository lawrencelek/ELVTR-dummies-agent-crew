Three districts · P5 mock-up

The three centres first, then each district as 3 × 3 screens of 800 × 640, in game art with live P5 traffic. Every highway screen has two escalators, on opposite sides at opposite ends. No trees or flower beds: concrete planters, monoliths and public sculptures. The P5 game is unchanged until these are approved.

The centres (B2)

The whole district. Tap a screen to see it at 2×.

Roads are planned across the whole district, so every screen edge lines up. Ground roads turn at 90° or 45° on fillets of world.roundaboutEntryRadius (96). Highways run square along a row (decision 152) at P5.2's width (160). Escalators: world.deckEscalators (2) per highway screen, world.deckEscalatorInset (64) from the screen edge. Colours follow the style guide: no red or yellow décor.
