Three districts · P5 mock-up

The three centres first, then each district as 3 × 3 screens of 800 × 640, in game art with live P5 traffic. Every tile reveals its own mechanic and landmark; roads and highways cross at 90° or 45°; every roundabout has four arms, and cars orbit on its inner lane when their exit is blocked. The P5 game is unchanged until these are approved.

The centres (B2)

The whole district. Tap a screen to see it at 2×.

Roads are planned across the whole district, so every screen edge lines up. Ground roads turn at 90° or 45° on fillets of world.roundaboutEntryRadius (96). Highways run across, down or at 45°, always crossing roads at 90° or 45°, at P5.2's width (160). Escalators: world.deckEscalators (2) per highway screen, world.deckEscalatorInset (64) from the screen edge. Colours follow the style guide: no red or yellow décor.

Edit 53: each district runs one traffic. A car crossing a screen edge drives on into the next screen at the same speed; at a junction or roundabout it picks its route there. Cars enter only where a road or highway crosses the district edge, one per 2 × world.district.<id>.interval per entry, and the district runs traffic.warmSeconds (30) before it is shown.
