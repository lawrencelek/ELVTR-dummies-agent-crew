The logic

  1. Roads keep their level; the ground moves. A Z1 road is a street on the hill and a bridge over the valley.
  2. Every road goes somewhere. Each runs edge to edge or ends at a junction.
  3. Roads follow the terrain. The arterial follows the valley; the ridge road the hilltop; the drive the shore. The tunnel takes the valley north through the hill.
  4. No ramps (D35). Valley and hill roads meet beyond the district. People climb by stairs, escalators, cliff houses and walkways.
  5. The circus is a real junction. The avenue meets the tunnel road and the beach road: the valley's only way north or to the water.
  6. The edges hold. A wall of buildings closes the top; the river closes the bottom. Only roads leave.

Rules

    Ramps (D35): how they would work

    • Lane: a ramp lane carries z from its foot to its head. A car's z interpolates along it.
    • Drawing: the art lift interpolates too, 0 to 18 art px per level.
    • Hits: today a hit needs the same level. On a ramp: |z car − z dummy| under the car's height (vehicles.*.height).
    • Drops: fall height is dummy z minus car z.
    • Network: the ramp joins a Z0 lane at its foot and a Z1 lane at its head. Lanes already link by endpoint.
    • Shadows: option 2 sees a ramp as steps; edges quantise.
    • New verticality: on-ramps to the highways, spiral car-park ramps, climbs up coves, dummies sliding down a ramp's pavement.
    • Here: a tunnel instead. Ramps logged as a future mechanic (D38).
