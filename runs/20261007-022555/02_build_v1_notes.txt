Implemented CR-003. In sim.js the Damage module now returns five zones and scores damage = min(maxPay, round(100 × zone share × lunge factor (5 or 1) × speed·mass/(138·1.0))). The CR-002 params and lunge-direction logic are removed, and every zone pays, including tail and rear corner. A killing hit now counts the write-off at once and enters mode "writeoff" with state.writeOff {t, duration 1.1, x, y}. That mode freezes everything and ignores confirm. When it ends it sets the card, or the Licensed ending at 11 bodies. index.html draws the write-off shake (14·(1−u)² px), the red-then-fading dummy and an 18-particle burst. Both effects read only state.writeOff. I found no specification problems. One note: the report signature stats now count rear contacts as "rear" for any zone behind the car, and they skip zero-damage contacts.
