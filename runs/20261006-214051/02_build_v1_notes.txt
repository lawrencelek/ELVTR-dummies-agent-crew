Ported the Testpad to a pure seeded sim (mulberry32 in state.rng, a separate cosmetic stream state.lineRng for report-line picks so spawn draws stay on the specified order) with the three levels, lane-based spawning, fresh-press lunge/confirm, easeOutCubic lunge, easeOutQuad recovery, write-off/level/ending flow, and a renderer that draws each level's roads, level name and number, and a recovery ring. I did not run either file, so none of it has been tested. Spec problems and additions: (1) The pure give-way rule can overlap cars, because a lower-serial car may drive into a higher-serial car that is already stopped across its path. I therefore (a) applied the touch backstop to every car against every other car, not only lower serials, and (b) made a car skip a yield when its own body is already inside the lower car's look-ahead and the two are not head-on. (2) Head-on collinear cars on level 3 would deadlock, so the yielding car sidesteps laterally at 70 px/s until the sweeps clear. (3) The walk/run ramp is held at 0 during the lunge and recovery so speed is exactly walk x f. (4) The Testpad hit-stop freeze is omitted. (5) Report bands use the level number (levels 1, 2 and 3 map to a, b and c). (6) The renderer sends confirm as a one-step pulse on each non-repeat keydown or pointer press, so held movement keys never dismiss a card.
