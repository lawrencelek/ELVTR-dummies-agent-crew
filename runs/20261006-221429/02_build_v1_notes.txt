I implemented CR-001. sim.js has the new PARAMS (masses, perMomentum, contactEps, pushClearance, shoulder). Damage is min(maxPay, round(perMomentum × closing × mass × face)), and severity is damage/maxPay. Every car move is truncated against all other cars, so cars never overlap. Lateral moves are barrier-limited and only go into free space. Swerve feasibility is checked at the commit line and every step while locked, and an infeasible swerve makes the car brake. Level 3 spawns wait on oncoming bands. The step order is dummy, cars, impacts (before push-out), push-out, hall clamp. index.html draws shoulders and barriers (barriers have gaps at the junction) and nothing extra on level 3. I did not run any of the automated checks or open the page.

Spec notes: (1) Margins grow as units learn, up to 190 px. The target lateral centre T is margin px from the lane, and the rule requires the whole car to stay inside the barriers, so learned cars often can't swerve and brake instead. This is faithful to the rule but may feel harsh. (2) I made the oncoming-band test reject only moves that reduce lateral distance, so a car already in overlap can still move away. (3) The lock still never releases, as in the released build. (4) "Inversely" is implemented as "directly", as the spec says, and still needs Lawrence to confirm.
