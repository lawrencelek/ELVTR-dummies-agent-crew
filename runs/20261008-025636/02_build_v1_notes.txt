Change 7 implemented in sim.js only (index.html is unchanged; it already drew the 320 px path rectangle, side wedge, aware cue and brake lights from car.braking). A locking car now stores its target lateral centre `lockT` (set at commit, updated on a hatch flip, cleared on release). The swerve counts as complete when |lateral centre − lockT| ≤ 2 px, measured at the start of the step. Until then the committed car's target is the unsure target min(0.5, B) × cruise × F. Once complete it returns to cruise. If the swerve is infeasible it brakes with B and no F. The lock is released only when the dummy's actual position is behind the car's rear edge by more than the dummy radius (a < −(length/2 + radius)).
